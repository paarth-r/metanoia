Your account

You need an account to track a reset or take part in groups. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what happens under your account. One person, one account.

Rules for what you post

Metanoia carries content other people can see: your username and display name, your plan and habit text if your plan is public or friends-visible, group messages, and group images. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Do not post:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or targeting of any person or group.
  • Hate speech, slurs, or content demeaning people on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, age, or sexual orientation.
  • Sexually explicit material, or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Content promoting self-harm, disordered eating, or dangerous behaviour.
  • Illegal content, or the promotion of illegal acts.
  • Spam, scams, impersonation, or another person's private information.

Messages are screened against a filter before they post, but the filter is not the whole system: reporting is.

Reporting and blocking

Every user profile and every group message can be reported from inside the app. Reports are reviewed and acted on, ordinarily within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed and the account behind it may be suspended or deleted without notice.

You can also block any user. A block is mutual in effect: neither of you sees the other's ledger, feed activity, or group messages afterwards.

Your content

What you write stays yours. You grant only the permission needed to run the service: to store your content and show it to the people your own visibility setting allows.

Ending it

You can delete your account at any time from Account, then Delete account. It is immediate and permanent. Accounts that break these rules may be suspended or removed.

No warranty

Metanoia is provided as is, without warranty. It is a habit tracker, not medical, psychological, or professional advice. It may be unavailable or lose data; keep your own record of anything you cannot afford to lose.
