Metanoia

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Metanoia is a thirty-day habit tracker you can run in public. This policy says exactly what it stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. It is short because the app collects little.

What is collected

  • Your email address, to create your account and sign you in. It is never shown to other users.
  • Your username and display name, chosen by you at signup. These are public: they appear on your profile, in feeds, and to group members.
  • What you write in the app: your plan name and intent, your daily non-negotiables and weekly targets, which days you ticked, your todos, and any messages you send in a group.
  • Group images, if you upload one.

There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of personal data. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health data is requested or collected.

Who can see it

Every plan carries a visibility you choose, and it is enforced by database row-level security rather than by the app:

  • Private - only you.
  • Friends - people whose friend request you accepted, and members of groups you joined.
  • Public - anyone, including signed-out visitors, at your profile page.

Group messages are visible to members of that group. Todos are always private to you: they are never shared, never appear in any feed, and no other account can read them.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database and handles authentication. The website is served by GitHub Pages. Both may process technical information such as IP addresses in the ordinary course of serving requests.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: Account, then Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your login, profile, plans, ticks, todos, group memberships, messages, friendships and feed activity. It cannot be undone and there is no recovery window. Messages you sent are removed along with your account.

Children

Metanoia is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above changes with it.
